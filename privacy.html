Privacy Policy

Nash Machine · Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains how the Nash Machine desktop application ("the app") accesses, uses, stores and shares information, including Google user data from your Gmail account. The app is provided by Nash Machine ("we").

1. How the app works

The app is installed and runs on your own Windows computer. You connect it to your own Gmail account using Google sign-in. The app talks directly to Gmail from your computer. We do not run servers that receive, store or process your Gmail data.

2. Google user data the app accesses

With your permission, the app accesses the following in the Gmail account you connect:

  • Email messages and threads between you and the clients you add to the app: sender, recipient, subject, date, message text, and whether a message bounced.
  • Drafts: the app reads reply drafts to find short commands you type for it, and may replace or delete those drafts after carrying out the command.
  • Labels: the app reads, adds and removes Gmail labels that it uses to track its work.
  • Your Gmail address, so the app knows which account it is connected to.

The app also sends email from your account to the clients you list.

3. How the app uses this data

The app uses Google user data only to provide its features to you:

  • sending the follow-up emails, reminders and documents you set up;
  • detecting client replies so follow-ups stop and you are notified;
  • carrying out commands you type in Gmail drafts or apply as labels;
  • detecting bounced emails and unsubscribe requests so those people are no longer emailed;
  • showing you a history of what was sent and received for each client.

The app does not use Google user data for advertising. We do not sell Google user data.

4. Where data is stored

Data the app keeps is stored on your own computer, in the app's data folder: your client list, send history, a short record of client replies, your settings, and your Google sign-in token. The sign-in token lets the app stay connected to your Gmail; it stays on your computer.

5. Sharing and transfer of data

We do not receive your Gmail data, and we do not share it with anyone. The app sends data to other services only in these cases, each of which you control:

  • Google (Gmail): to read and send email in your account.
  • Telegram, only if you connect your own Telegram bot: the app sends you notifications, which can include a client's email address and a short status message.
  • Your own Google Apps Script, only if you set up this optional sign-in method: the app asks a script in your own Google account for a sign-in token. No email content is sent to it.
  • A translation service (Google Translate), only if you mark a client as Spanish-speaking: the text of the email being sent is translated. Names, amounts, dates, links and email addresses are replaced with placeholders before translation and restored afterwards.

No human at our company reads your Gmail data. We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as described above to provide the app's features to you.

6. Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google Workspace APIs is not used to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

7. How data is protected

  • Data stays on your computer and is protected by your Windows account.
  • The app's dashboard is only reachable from your own computer unless you choose to open it to your network, and it can be protected with a password.
  • Connections to Gmail and other services use encryption in transit (HTTPS).
  • We recommend turning on device encryption on the computer that runs the app.

8. Keeping and deleting data

  • Data is kept on your computer until you delete it.
  • You can delete all business data at any time with the app's "Fresh Start" reset, or by uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder.
  • You can remove the app's access to your Gmail at any time in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps and services.
  • Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you have questions, contact us and we will help.

9. Your clients' information

You decide which clients are added to the app and what is sent to them. You are responsible for having the right to email them. Every email sent to a client includes a way to unsubscribe, and the app stops emailing people who unsubscribe.

10. Changes to this policy

If the way the app uses Google user data changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
