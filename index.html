Nash Machine

A desktop email assistant that sends follow-up emails to your existing clients and helps you answer their replies.

What the app does

Nash Machine is a Windows desktop application for businesses and service providers who email their own existing clients. It runs on your own computer and works through your own Gmail account. It helps you:

  • send follow-up emails and reminders to your existing clients on a schedule you control;
  • send documents such as agreements and receipts as email attachments;
  • notice when a client replies, so follow-ups stop and you can answer;
  • reply quickly using your own saved email templates;
  • stop emailing anyone who unsubscribes or replies STOP.

Why the app asks for access to your Gmail

The app needs permission to use your Gmail account so that it can do the work above on your behalf. It uses this access to:

  • send email from your account to the clients you list;
  • read replies from those clients, so it can stop follow-ups and show you who answered;
  • read and update your drafts and labels, so you can give the app instructions from inside Gmail (for example, typing a short command in a reply draft);
  • detect bounced emails, so it stops emailing addresses that do not work.

The app runs on your computer. Your email data is stored on your computer, not on servers run by the developer. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Who it is for

Any business or service provider that wants to automate follow-up email with its existing clients while keeping a person in control of what is sent.
